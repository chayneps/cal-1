cal-1

Unix cal(1)-inspired dropdown calendar for Google Chrome with command line prompt styling, 4-month 2×2 overview, hover schedule inspection, and Google Calendar & Tasks synchronization.

4-Month 2×2 Overview

Glance across four consecutive months with Thai daily color coding, blinking today indicator, and smooth directional scan transitions.

Hover Schedule Split

Hover over any date to immediately inspect scheduled events, multi-calendar badges, tasks, and public holidays.

Google Calendar & Tasks

Connect your Google Account to view events from your personal, work, birthday, and task calendars directly in your browser toolbar.

100% Local & Private

Communicates directly with official Google APIs from your browser. All cached events and preferences remain strictly on your device.
